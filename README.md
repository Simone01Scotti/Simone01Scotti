### 🛠️ What I do

I'm a developer at **Oncode**, where I work on **Java / Spring Boot microservices** and **Angular** front-ends.
Day to day that means event-driven services with RabbitMQ and Spring Cloud Stream, PostgreSQL with Liquibase migrations, and Angular apps talking GraphQL through Apollo.

### 🌱 What I'm building

- **Compitum** — an open-source, self-hosted hub to organise and supervise AI coding agents (Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode) and their sessions across projects and devices. 
- Small full-stack sites for local businesses and associations, plus the occasional Android side project.
